# Terrarium

A sealed glass terrarium on your screen, alive. Water evaporates under the
lamp, condenses on the cool glass and runs back down; plants grow toward the
light and roots toward water; tiny creatures graze, decompose and evolve.
Nothing enters the jar but light — and the books always balance.

**Status: in development** (milestone M0). Built in the open by Faisal
Chishti with a team of Claude Code agents. Read how it is designed in
[docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md) and follow progress in
[docs/TASKS.md](docs/TASKS.md) and [docs/checkpoints/](docs/checkpoints/).

Terrarium is the successor to Veridia, an earlier deterministic world
simulation (see DESIGN §1.1).
